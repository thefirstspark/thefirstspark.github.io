It started with a code no one could read.

In 2020, my mother's murder — cold for 29 years — was finally cracked. I didn't solve it. Detectives did, using forensic DNA technology that could finally read what had been sitting in plain sight the whole time.

The killer had been living across the street.

That broke something open in me. If the truth could hide that long — encoded in data no one had the tools to read until the right technology arrived — what else is written in code we walk past every day? Our numbers. Our patterns. Our lives.

I didn't start a psychic hotline. I started building. 60+ consciousness tools later, The First Spark exists to answer one question: what if you could finally read your own code?
