◈   007 — Straight Answers

The questions worth asking.

What if the round doesn't fill completely?
The plan degrades in priority order, not proportionally. The model ships first — it's the moat. Advertising scales to whatever remains. A partial raise slows the timeline; it doesn't change the destination. Burn stays at zero either way.
Why train a standalone model instead of using an API?
An API wrapper is a feature anyone can copy in a weekend, priced at someone else's margin, living at someone else's mercy. Owned weights trained on a proprietary corpus are an asset — one that appreciates with every member interaction and can't be replicated without the canon.
What exactly am I buying with a SAFE?
A YC-standard post-money SAFE at a $2M valuation cap — your investment converts to equity at the cap (12.5% for the full $250K) or better when a priced round happens. Full terms are in the deck and deal documents. Ask for them.
