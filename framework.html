L0

Thesis

Root layer · inherited by every layer below

The Thesis is the one claim the whole system rests on. It is not a technique or a chart. It is the statement that makes every layer beneath it coherent: reality is programmable, and consciousness is the code. Nothing lower in the stack may contradict it, and any ruling that would is rejected at the Thesis rather than patched further down.

It also sets the register. The canon is written in the telling, an account rather than an assertion, and read through what we call the Sim Lens, a computational voice that describes the system as a system. Neither register claims the other is wrong. They are two ways of reading the same structure.

L1

The Selector Model

4 domains · quadrants of the wheel

The Selector Model divides lived experience into four domains: Physics, Metaphysical, Relational and Temporal. They are the system's coarsest sort. Before an archetype, before a number, before a color, an experience belongs to a domain. On the archetype wheel the four domains are the four quadrants, which is what lets structures from different layers point at the same region without collapsing into each other.

Domains describe where work is felt. They never name who is doing it. That distinction is what keeps the archetype layer and the number layer independent.

DL-010 · the firewall

A karmic debt can say which domain its work lives in. It can never say which archetype you are. Numbers and archetypes are kept on opposite sides of a firewall, and the knot sector is deliberately built to stop at the domain.

L2

The KJP Core Code

3 verbs · the cycle everything runs on

Three verbs, in order, run continuously. They are the system's smallest working unit: the motion a person is always somewhere inside of, and the sequence the Engine reverses when it is read as a life rather than as a construction.

01 · Witness

You see it

The pattern becomes visible as a pattern. Not as fate, not as fault. Nothing can be worked with until it has been witnessed.

02 · Release

You put it down

The witnessed thing stops being load-bearing. Release is the hinge of the cycle: the Mirror belongs here, and the wheel's balance depends on it.

03 · Select

You choose again

With the weight down, a choice is actually available. Select feeds straight back into Witness. The cycle has no terminal state.

DL-001 · Mirror = Release

Resolved 16 Aug 2026. The Mirror sits under Release, not Select. This preserves the 4 / 4 / 4 distribution of archetypes across the three verbs and keeps the two-bridge rule intact. The archetype wheel is being redrawn to match, and the earlier "discovered resonance / crossover" narrative is retired.

L3

The 12 Archetypes

12 figures · four to each verb · gift and shadow

Twelve archetypes distribute evenly across the Core Code, four to Witness, four to Release, four to Select, and sit on the wheel inside the Selector domains. Each one carries two faces: a gift, the capacity it grants when it is being lived cleanly, and a shadow, the same capacity turned against its holder.

The gift and the shadow are not two archetypes. They are one archetype seen from either side of a release.

L5

The Color Codex

11 tiers · permanent light, seasonal weather

Every person has one native light: a single tier of the eleven-tier Codex. It is permanent. It is not re-cast each year, it is not earned, and it is the sole palette from which that person's Soul Pattern is drawn.

The Personal Year is real, but it is not identity. It is the season: the year's light, weather rather than self. It belongs in written and annual readings only and is never rendered onto the pattern. Master numbers remain visitations, not tiers.

DL-008 v1.1 · The Light and the Season

A person's color never changes. The native light is permanent identity and the only palette the pattern draws from. The Personal Year is reframed as the season, the year's light, weather and not identity, and never touches the rendered pattern.

DL-009 · Radiant Number

Color is no longer derived from Life Path alone. It comes from Life Path and Expression summed and reduced together, so different roads can share a light. That is what made sense of people with different Life Paths reading as the same color. The tier table and tier names are unchanged; only the input formula moved.

L6

Numerology

2 inputs · 4 debts · 8 conditions

Two values are computed, and only two. The Life Path comes from the birth date: the Road, the circumstance a person arrives into. The Expression comes from the full birth name: the Vessel, the capacity they arrive with. Together, reduced, they give the Radiant Number that sets the color tier.

Four karmic debts, 13, 14, 16 and 19, are checked, and checked against the Life Path and Expression only. Because each debt can arrive by either carrier, there are eight karmic conditions in total, and the carrier changes what the debt means: a debt on the Road is something met, a debt on the Vessel is something held.

The Engine

5 movements · the stack told as a sequence

The stack describes what the parts are. The Engine describes what they do, in order: five movements from approach to emergence. Read forwards, it is how a pattern is made. Read backwards, through the Core Code, it is how a life is lived.

Movement I

Approach

Something arrives at the system and is met.

Movement II

Inscription

It is written in. Name and date become value.

Movement III

Refraction

The single value splits into light.

Movement IV

Wheel & Gates

The light is placed: quadrant, sector, threshold.

Movement V

Emergence

The pattern stands, and can be seen.

The reversal

Living is the Engine run backwards. Where construction moves approach → emergence, a person moves emergence → approach, one Witness–Release–Select turn at a time.
