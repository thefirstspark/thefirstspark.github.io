005 · Before you buy

Quick answers.

How is this different from the free pattern?

The free generator calculates your Life Path, Expression, Radiant Number and Codex tier, and draws them. The Soul Map reads them: what they mean for decisions, relationships and your next move, plus every layer the pattern does not show. See the table above.

Will my map change next year?

Your core numbers, your light and your archetype do not change. The Current Cycle section is the season, and it moves with the year. That is by design: DL-008, the Light and the Season.

What do I need to send?

Birth date and full birth name as on your birth certificate. That is the input. Time and city make the celestial layer sharper if you have them.

Can I gift it?

Yes. Pay, then submit their birth details instead of yours.

Is this a subscription?

No. $22 once. The page stays up.

Where do the rules come from?

Every map conforms to the First Spark Framework and the public Spark Canon. Nothing on your page is arbitrary, and the same inputs always produce the same map.
